My Responsibilities

My part of the application helps students share their preferences, compare their choices with teammates, and reach agreements.

My 10 Screens
1. Communication Preferences: Students choose how they prefer to communicate with teammates.
2. Weekly Availability: Students select the days and times they are available.
3. Preferred Roles: Students choose up to three roles they would like to have in the project.
4. Deadline Expectations: Students choose how strictly they prefer to follow deadlines.
5. Quality Expectations: Students select what matters most to them about the quality of the project.
6. Preference Comparison: Students compare their preferences with their teammates' choices.
7. Disagreement Overview: Students see where their preferences are different.
8. Create Proposal: Students suggest solutions to disagreements.
9. Review Proposal: Students review a proposal and can approve it or request changes.
10. Agreement Confirmation: Students see a confirmation after reaching an agreement.
