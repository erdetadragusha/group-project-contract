
# Deliverable 3 – Persona Research Questions

Student: Erdeta Dragusha
Date: November 22, 2026

## Research Goal

Understand how students communicate, divide responsibilities, and make decisions during group projects.

## Interview Questions

1. How do you usually communicate with your group members?
2. How do you decide who is responsible for each task?
3. What problems do you experience when organizing group meetings?
4. How do you keep track of deadlines?
5. What happens when group members disagree?
6. How do you make sure everyone agrees with a decision?
7. Have you ever had problems with unclear responsibilities?
8. Would using an application in your preferred language make group work easier?

## Features to Research

- Communication preferences
- Availability and scheduling
- Roles and responsibilities
- Deadlines
- Quality expectations
- Disagreement management
- Proposals and reviews
- Final agreement confirmation

## Next Steps

- Interview potential users.
- Record their real answers.
- Use the evidence to prepare two personas.
- Clearly mark any assumptions that have not been verified.
